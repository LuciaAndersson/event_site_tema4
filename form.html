How It Works
  1. Personalize your ticket – Fill out the form with your details to create your own unique event ticket.
  2. Receive your ticket – Once you have completed the form, your personal ticket will be ready.
  3. Download your ticket – Save your digital ticket to your device and bring it with you to the event.
  4. Check in at the entrance – When you arrive, simply give your name to our event staff. Make sure the name on your ticket matches the name you provide at the entrance.
  5. Get your printed ticket – Our staff will print your personalized ticket for you. Pin it to your outfit, step inside and enjoy the exhibition!
