Explore the event

Sustainable Fashion & Clothing

Creative Knit Expo explores how fashion can become more sustainable through recycled materials, upcycling and thoughtful design. The exhibition showcases how knitwear and textile techniques can transform discarded materials into unique garments, encouraging a more conscious approach to fashion.

Design & Craftmanship

Behind every artwork is a creative process shaped by experimentation, patience and craftsmanship. From choosing materials and exploring colours to knitting, stitching and shaping, each piece reflects the time, skill and imagination behind handmade textile art.

Textile Interiors & Sculptural Art

Textiles are not only made to be worn but can also transform interior spaces. Creative Knit Expo presents handmade rugs, decorative pieces and sculptural installations that explore texture, colour and form, blurring the boundaries between craft, design and contemporary art.
